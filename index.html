A little about me

A story still unfolding.

I come from Ethiopia, and the values I grew up with continue to shape the way I see family, faith, and responsibility. Today, I’m building a life in Columbus, Ohio, where I work, learn, serve my community, and raise my daughter.

My path has taken me from distribution and operational excellence to earning a degree in cybersecurity. The achievements matter to me, but they make the most sense as part of a larger story: a story of perseverance, gratitude, and the people who have walked beside me.

The heart of my life

Family is one of my greatest gifts.

My daughter and my younger brother remind me every day how much I have to be thankful for.

My younger brother

Yared

Yared is everything I could hope for in a brother, and almost perfect in my eyes. He is intelligent, thoughtful, and hardworking. Since we were kids, he has always been at the top of his class. He graduated from the University of Maryland with a degree in electrical engineering and is now a manager at American Electric Power. I’m proud of him, and deeply grateful to have him as my brother.

Being Liya’s father

Fatherhood changed how I see life.

My daughter, Liya, has changed the way I look at life in general. Being her father has given me a deeper sense of purpose and gratitude. God is my priority, and I believe it is my duty to help instill that faith in my daughter as I raise her. I want my life to reflect the love, guidance, and values I hope to pass on.

I have many reasons to thank God. When I think about Yared and Liya, I know that my God loves me.Samra
What grounds me

My faith comes first.

I am an Ethiopian Orthodox Christian, and my relationship with God is the foundation I return to. My faith guides the way I try to live, the way I care for my family, and the values I hope to pass on to Liya. Serving and volunteering with my church community is one way I put that care into practice.

I’m grateful for the community and fellowship I have found at St. Gabriel Church in Columbus.

Gratitude changes how I see the life I have been given.Faith, family, and service are not separate parts of my story. They belong together.
