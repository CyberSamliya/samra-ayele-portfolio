03

My brother Yared, fatherhood, and faith

My brother Yared is everything I could hope for in a brother, and honestly, he's close to perfect in my eyes. He's been at the top of his class since we were kids, intelligent and hardworking in a way that never surprised anyone who knew him growing up. He earned his degree in electrical engineering from the University of Maryland and is now a manager at American Electric Power. Watching him become the person he is today is one of the quiet joys of my life.

When I think about Yared and my daughter Liya, I'm reminded of just how deeply God loves me.

I have so many reasons to thank God, and they are two of the clearest blessings in my life. Becoming Liya's father changed the way I see everything. She's 8 now, and being her dad has given me a different sense of purpose than anything else I've done. God comes first in my life, and I believe it's my responsibility to help instill that same faith in her as she grows. I don't take that lightly, it's one of the things I care most about getting right.
