The Fasts of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church

Fasting isn't just tradition in our Church, it's a spiritual discipline tied to specific moments in our faith. There are seven major fasting periods each year:

Fast2026 datesWhat it commemorates
Great Lent (Abiy Tsom)Feb 16 – Apr 1155 days of preparation before Fasika (Easter), including Holy Week
Fast of the Apostles~Jun 1 – Jul 12The apostles fasting and praying before setting out to preach the Gospel
Fast of the Assumption (Filseta)Aug 7 – 21/22Honoring the Virgin Mary and her assumption
Fast of Nineveh (Tsome Nenewe)A few weeks before LentThe prophet Jonah's preaching and Nineveh's repentance
Advent / Fast of the ProphetsNov 25, 2026 – Jan 7, 2027The prophets who foretold Christ's birth
Wednesday & Friday fastsEvery week, year-roundChrist's betrayal (Wed) and crucifixion (Fri)

Dates above are calculated from Fasika (Easter) 2026 and the Ethiopian calendar; exact observance can vary slightly by parish, so please confirm against St. Gabriel's own calendar.

My Brother Yared, Fatherhood, and Faith

My brother Yared is everything I could hope for in a brother, and honestly, he's close to perfect in my eyes. He's been at the top of his class since we were kids, intelligent and hardworking in a way that never surprised anyone who knew him growing up. He earned his degree in electrical engineering from the University of Maryland and is now a manager at American Electric Power. Watching him become the person he is today is one of the quiet joys of my life.

I have so many reasons to thank God, and when I think about Yared and my daughter Liya, I'm reminded of just how deeply He loves me. They are two of the clearest blessings in my life.

Becoming Liya's father changed the way I see everything. She's 8 now, and being her dad has given me a different sense of purpose than anything else I've done. God comes first in my life, and I believe it's my responsibility to help instill that same faith in her as she grows. I don't take that lightly, it's one of the things I care most about getting right.
